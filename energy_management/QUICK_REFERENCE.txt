┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎯 FEATURE 1: ANOMALY DETECTION                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

URL: /anomaly-detection/
Menu: "Anomaly Detection"

PURPOSE:
  Detect abnormal energy usage patterns indicating theft or faults

ALGORITHMS:
  • Isolation Forest (scikit-learn)
  • Autoencoder Neural Network (PyTorch)

INPUT PARAMETERS:
  • Energy Consumption (kWh)    → Normal range: 30-70 kWh
  • Temperature (°C)             → e.g., 25.5
  • Humidity (%)                 → e.g., 60.0
  • Occupancy Status             → Occupied/Unoccupied

OUTPUT:
  • Anomaly/Normal classification
  • Alert messages
  • Energy theft detection
  • Recommendations

EXAMPLE:
  Input:  Consumption=150 kWh, Temp=25°C, Humidity=60%, Occupied
  Output: ⚠️ ANOMALY DETECTED! Consumption exceeds normal by 200%

FILES:
  • Backend: users/ml_models.py → AnomalyDetector class
  • View: users/views.py → anomaly_detection_view()
  • Template: assets/templates/users/anomaly_detection.html

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚡ FEATURE 2: SMART DEVICE SCHEDULING                                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

URL: /device-scheduling/
Menu: "Device Scheduling"

PURPOSE:
  Automatically schedule appliances for optimal energy efficiency

TECHNOLOGY:
  • Time-series analysis
  • Energy price optimization
  • Occupancy-based scheduling

SUPPORTED DEVICES:
  • Air Conditioner (AC)
  • Heater
  • Water Heater
  • Washing Machine
  • Dishwasher
  • EV Charger

ENERGY PRICING:
  • Off-Peak:  12AM-6AM, 10PM-12AM  → $0.08/kWh (Cheapest)
  • Mid-Peak:  6AM-10AM, 4PM-10PM   → $0.10/kWh (Medium)
  • Peak:      10AM-4PM             → $0.12/kWh (Most Expensive)

WORKFLOW:
  1. Add Device → Select device, preferred time, duration
  2. Optimize → System calculates optimal schedule
  3. View Results → See cost savings and recommended times

EXAMPLE:
  Device: AC
  Preferred: 6 PM (Peak hour - $0.12/kWh)
  Optimized: 10 PM (Off-peak - $0.08/kWh)
  Savings: 33% cost reduction

FILES:
  • Backend: users/ml_models.py → DeviceScheduler class
  • View: users/views.py → device_scheduling_view()
  • Template: assets/templates/users/device_scheduling.html

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📊 FEATURE 3: ENERGY CONSUMPTION PREDICTION                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

URL: /energy-prediction/
Menu: "Energy Forecast"

PURPOSE:
  Predict future energy consumption for planning and optimization

ALGORITHMS:
  • Random Forest Regressor (100 estimators)
  • XGBoost (Gradient boosting)
  • LSTM Neural Network (2 layers, 64 units)
  • Ensemble Method (combines all three)

PREDICTION FACTORS:
  • Weather conditions (temperature, humidity, wind speed)
  • Time of day and day of week
  • Historical consumption patterns
  • Occupancy status
  • Seasonal variations

WORKFLOW:
  1. Train Model → Click "Train Model" (first time only)
  2. Predict → Click "Predict Next 24 Hours"
  3. View Results → See hourly predictions, costs, tips

OUTPUT:
  • 24-hour hourly predictions
  • Total predicted consumption (kWh)
  • Estimated total cost ($)
  • Energy-saving tips
  • Temperature forecast

EXAMPLE:
  Prediction: 1,200 kWh over 24 hours
  Cost: $144.00
  Peak Hour (2 PM): 65 kWh at $0.12/kWh = $7.80
  Off-Peak (3 AM): 35 kWh at $0.08/kWh = $2.80

FILES:
  • Backend: users/ml_models.py → EnergyPredictor, LSTMEnergyPredictor
  • View: users/views.py → energy_prediction_view()
  • Template: assets/templates/users/energy_prediction.html
